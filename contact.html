What to include in your message

You don't need a formal brief. A few details are enough to start:

  • What your business does or what you want to build.
  • The problem you're trying to solve.
  • Features or outcomes you have in mind.
  • Your preferred timeline, if you have one.
  • A rough budget range, if already decided.

We'll review the requirement, ask any necessary questions and discuss a realistic scope before work begins.
